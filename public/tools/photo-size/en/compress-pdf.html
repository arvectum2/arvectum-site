How to compress a PDF to 1 MB or 500 KB on iPhone

A website says your PDF is too large? Set a target size in KB or MB and reduce the document on your iPhone without uploading it to an online PDF converter.

Get the free iPhone app ↗

Step-by-step

  1. Install the free Photo & PDF Size app by Arvectum.
  2. Open the PDF by file size mode and choose your PDF from Files.
  3. Select 500 KB, 1 MB or enter your own maximum file size.
  4. Compress, inspect the result and save or share the PDF.

PDF quality trade-offs

Heavy PDF compression can turn each page into an image. Text may then become impossible to select, copy or search. Check readability and the recipient’s requirements before uploading. Some PDFs cannot be significantly reduced without a quality trade-off.

Privacy

PDF content is processed on your iPhone. Internet may be used to load ads, but files are not uploaded for conversion.

FAQ

Can I make a PDF exactly 1 MB?

Set a maximum size and inspect the output. The achievable result depends on the source file.

Do I need an online PDF converter?

No. File processing is done on the iPhone.

Will PDF text remain selectable?

Not always. High compression may rasterize pages and remove searchable text.
