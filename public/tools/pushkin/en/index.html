Arvectum Tools · iPhone

Pushkin — save the notifications you choose

Use your own Apple Shortcuts automations to save selected iPhone notifications into a searchable local history. No Arvectum account required.

Get Pushkin on the App Store ↗

For iPhone running iOS 27 or later. Automations require the appropriate iOS permissions.

How it works

  1. Choose apps and set up the Shortcuts automations with Pushkin’s guidance.
  2. Enabled automations deliver selected notifications into your local history.
  3. Search, review and delete saved notifications on your iPhone.

Features

A limitation of iOS

iOS does not let third-party apps silently read all push notifications. Pushkin relies on Apple Shortcuts automations that you configure and enable. Coverage depends on iOS capabilities and your settings.

Privacy

Notification history stays on the device, with no required account or cloud sync. Notification content is not shared with ad networks.
