Frequently asked questions

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. UhWhat? is a fully functional iPhone voice recorder on iOS 17 and later.

Does recording work offline?

Yes. The original audio is saved locally. On-device transcription depends on Apple language asset availability.

What happens if my Watch is offline?

Audio stays queued on Apple Watch and syncs to the iPhone after reconnection. Background transfer timing depends on watchOS and iOS.
