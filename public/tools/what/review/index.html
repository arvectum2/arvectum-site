What the demonstration shows

One-tap capture home, a searchable recording library, playback of locally stored original M4A audio, and an editable transcript. All notes displayed were created solely as sample data for App Review.

Physical-device walkthrough

On iPhone, tap the central record button, allow microphone access, speak, and choose Stop and save. In My recordings, play audio, transcribe if on-device Speech is available, edit text, search, archive and export. Apple Watch app and complication are optional: the Watch retains audio through disconnection and sends it when WatchConnectivity becomes available. No sign-in or network is needed for capture.
